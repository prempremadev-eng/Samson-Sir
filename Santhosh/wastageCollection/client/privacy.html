🧹 CleanCity — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

CleanCity is an internal tool used by authorised supervisors and administrators to record daily waste collection for each collection vehicle. It is not open to the public.

Information we collect

  • When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address.
  • Waste collection figures you enter (date, vehicle, wet and dry waste in kg).

How we use it

  • Your email is used only to check whether you are on the list of authorised users and which location you manage.
  • Collection figures are used to prepare daily and location-wise waste reports.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. Data is stored on Zoho Catalyst servers.

Removing your data

An administrator can remove your access at any time. To ask for your account to be deleted, contact premprema90801@gmail.com.
