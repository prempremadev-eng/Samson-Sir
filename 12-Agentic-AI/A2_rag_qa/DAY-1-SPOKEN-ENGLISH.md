# Day 1 — Spoken English Practice 🗣️ (13 Oct 2026)

Read each line **out loud** 3 times.

## Talking about what I built

1. "Today I built the first part of a RAG system."
2. "My program reads three PDF files and keeps the text with the file name."
3. "I split the text into small pieces called chunks."
4. "I tried two chunking strategies: fixed-size and recursive."
5. "Fixed-size chunking cuts every 500 characters, even in the middle of a word."
6. "Recursive chunking tries to cut at paragraphs, lines and spaces first."

## Talking about errors

7. "I got a NameError because I forgot the quotes around the path."
8. "Python glued two file names together because a comma was missing."
9. "My function returned too early because `return` was inside the loop."
10. "The error showed line 12, but the real mistake was on line 4."
11. "An extra star symbol split my text into sixty pieces."

## Talking about how I feel

12. "I'm tired now, so I'll stop here and continue tomorrow."
13. "I learned that small symbols like commas and colons really matter."
14. "Next time, I'll check whether the numbers make sense."
